Degree-variance correction for independent sets

A proved lower bound showing that, at fixed average degree, degree irregularity can improve the guaranteed independent-set size.

Proof completed Finite verification passed Derived from Caro–Wei Historical novelty not established

The result

Let G be a finite simple graph with n vertices, degree sequence d₁,…,dₙ, average degree d̄, maximum degree Δ, and degree variance σ²d.

Proved lower bound
α(G) ≥ n/(d̄+1) + nσ²d/((d̄+1)²(Δ+1))

The first term is the usual average-degree lower bound. The correction term is nonnegative and grows with degree variance.

At fixed average degree, degree irregularity improves this guaranteed independent-set lower bound.

A stronger retained-degree form

Before replacing each denominator by Δ+1, the proof gives a stronger form that keeps each vertex’s own degree.

Stronger form
α(G) ≥ n/(d̄+1) + 1/(d̄+1)² · Σv((d(v)−d̄)²/(d(v)+1))

Proof

Set a=d̄+1 and xᵢ=dᵢ+1. The key step is an exact identity, not an approximation.

Exact identity
1/xᵢ = 1/a − (xᵢ−a)/a² + (xᵢ−a)²/(a²xᵢ)
01
Sum over all vertices. Since Σᵢ(dᵢ−d̄)=0, the linear term cancels.
02
Therefore Σᵢ1/(dᵢ+1) = n/(d̄+1) + 1/(d̄+1)² · Σᵢ((dᵢ−d̄)²/(dᵢ+1)).
03
Since dᵢ+1 ≤ Δ+1, the correction is at least nσ²d/((d̄+1)²(Δ+1)).
04
Apply Caro–Wei: α(G) ≥ Σᵢ1/(dᵢ+1). The claimed bound follows.

Novelty status

The supplied research trace says the formula was proposed before a targeted literature search. That search located the classical Caro–Wei bound and related work but did not find an exact match.

No world-first claim is made. Not finding an exact match in a targeted search does not establish historical novelty, especially because this result follows from Caro–Wei by a short algebraic argument.
